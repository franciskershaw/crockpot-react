# CFE-055 — Account settings page (Epic 7: Account Management)

A `/account` page, reached from the avatar menu, with three sections:
Profile, Password and Delete account. Pairs with `crockpot-go` `CROC-051`
(profile, password) and `CROC-030` (deletion), which hold the API decisions.
AI-driven. Grilled 2026-10-07 as one epic across both repos. **Blocked on
designs:** the brief below goes to Claude Design, and the PNGs plus a spec
dump land in `../screenshots/account/`. A design review then reopens only
the decisions marked as design-shaped.

## Current state (read 2026-10-07)

- `UserMenu` (`src/components/nav/UserMenu.tsx`) is an avatar dropdown
  showing name, email and "Log out". It sits in `SiteHeader` at every
  breakpoint. `MobileTabBar` has no account item.
- `endSession(queryClient)` ends a session and lists account deletion as a
  future caller (`CFE-050`). `startSession(queryClient, accessToken)` is the
  only way into a signed-in session (`CFE-002b`).
- `RecipeHero.tsx:107` hides the byline when `createdByName` is null.
- `authSchemas.ts:40` requires a trimmed name of at least one character.
- No account designs exist; `screenshots/auth/` is login, register,
  forgot and reset only.

## Decisions

**Fixed by the API, and the designs don't reopen them:**

1. **Name:** trimmed, 1–50 characters; `PATCH /me` → 400 `invalid_name`.
   The frontend schema matches, and register's schema gains the
   50-character maximum.
2. **Change password** returns a fresh access token and cookie; the page
   passes it to `startSession`. Other devices are signed out.
3. **A wrong password returns 403 `invalid_password`** on both password
   change and delete, shown inline. It's never treated as an expired session.
4. **`/me` gains `authProvider: "password" | "google"`** and loses `image`.
   The page branches on `authProvider`.
5. **Delete succeeds with 204:** `endSession`, navigate to `/`, and show the
   toast "Your account has been deleted."
6. **ADMIN can't delete their own account** (403
   `admin_cannot_self_delete`); the page disables the action up front
   instead of waiting for the error.

**Design-shaped (agreed now, and the mockups may revise them):**

7. **Entry point:** an "Account settings" item in the avatar menu, above
   "Log out". No tab-bar item.
8. **One page with three stacked sections,** each with its own save button
   and errors: Profile (name editable, email read-only), Password (current,
   new), Delete account (last, styled as dangerous).
9. **Google accounts:** the Password section stays, with no form, and reads
   "You sign in with Google, so there's no password to change here." Next to
   the email in Profile: "Signed in with Google".
10. **Deletion happens in a confirmation dialog.** It says what is deleted
    (menu, shopping list, favourites, regulars, unpublished recipes) and what
    stays (published recipes, without your name). It asks for the password,
    or for a Google account the typed email; the delete button stays
    disabled until that's filled in or matches. A wrong password shows inline
    and the dialog stays open.
11. **Success feedback:** a name save shows the toast "Name updated" and
    updates the avatar initials. A password change shows "Password changed"
    and clears the fields.

## Acceptance criteria

Visual criteria V1–V16 are under the design review above. Behaviour:

- [ ] "Account settings" in the avatar menu navigates to `/account`;
      the route is wrapped in `RequireAuth`, like the other signed-in
      routes.
- [ ] A name save sends the trimmed value, rejects 0 or 51+ characters
      before sending, and on success updates the session's user (avatar
      initials change) with no reload.
- [ ] A password change calls `startSession` with the returned token. A 403
      shows inline on Current password and doesn't sign the user out.
- [ ] Google accounts see no password form; ADMIN sees deletion disabled.
- [ ] The delete dialog's button stays disabled until the password is filled
      in, or the typed email matches the account's email (case-insensitive,
      trimmed). A 403 keeps the dialog open with an inline error. A 204 calls
      `endSession`, navigates to `/`, and shows the toast.

## Non-goals

- Changing email, a session list, sign out everywhere, avatars.
- A dedicated "goodbye" screen.

## Verification

- **Visual:** each piece on screen in the dev app, compared by the founder
  against its `screenshots/account/` PNG (desktop and mobile). Blocked until
  the PNGs and spec dump exist.
- **Logic:** Vitest + Testing Library on the forms (name rule, the 403
  inline path, delete-button enablement, the `startSession` / `endSession`
  calls).
- **Interactive (founder):** change your password signed in on two
  browsers; the second gets signed out within 15 minutes. Delete a
  throwaway password account and a throwaway Google account end to end.
  Confirm an admin account shows deletion disabled.
